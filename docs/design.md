# Design

An editorial research workspace: warm ivory canvas, dark green ink, restrained orange accent, generous reading space, serif headlines, and accessible sans-serif controls. Evidence and the selected provider stay visible. No fabricated usage metrics or sample answers masquerade as real results.

Desktop: persistent narrow session sidebar; chat in the middle; collapsible artifact pane on the right. Empty state offers three concrete growth questions and explains the archive boundary. Composer supports Answer, Essay, Markdown, and HTML with explicit labels. Provider choice is visible above the conversation.

States: service check, empty archive, ready, retrieving, generating, complete, canceled, failed. Show truthful progress rather than invented percentages. Citation cards expand to exact source excerpts. Artifact preview/source toggle and download support evaluation. Essay word count is visible. Failed generation keeps a recoverable conversation with an error notice.

On small screens sidebar becomes a drawer and artifacts occupy a separate tab. Inputs remain reachable without horizontal scrolling. Semantic buttons, visible focus rings, labeled controls, readable contrast, keyboard submission (Enter; Shift+Enter newline), aria-live status, and reduced-motion support are required. Auto-scroll only when the reader is already near the bottom.

UI manual verification: create two chats, switch/reload, follow-up, missing cloud key, stop generation, narrow viewport, keyboard navigation, view/download both artifact types, and malicious artifact isolation.
