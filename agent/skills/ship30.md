# Ship 30 writing skill
Source: https://www.ship30for30.com/post/how-to-write-an-atomic-essay-a-beginners-guide (read 2026-09-18).
The source teaches a single focused idea, a defined reader, a deliberate headline/hook, an organized body, and editing for easy reading. Its atomic format is 250 words. This assignment explicitly requests an expanded 1,250-word adaptation.

Execution rubric:
- Choose one clear thesis that answers the user's question for a growth PM.
- Write a specific benefit-led headline, then a 2–3 sentence hook grounded in the supplied evidence.
- Develop 4–5 connected sections: problem, insight, implications, practical application, takeaway.
- Target 1,250 words (acceptable range 1,100–1,400). Budget roughly 100 introduction, 950 body, 200 conclusion.
- Use Markdown H2/H3, short paragraphs of 1–3 sentences, selective bold anchors, and one useful list.
- Attribute every factual claim to supplied [S1] source identifiers. Never invent quotes, metrics, companies, case studies, or anecdotes to fill space.
- Distinguish your proposed application from a guest's actual advice. If evidence cannot support the requested depth, explain the limitation instead of padding.
- Finish with a concrete checklist a reader can apply this week.
- Edit once for repetitive claims, readability, traceability, and length before emitting the final essay.
